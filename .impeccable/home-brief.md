# Surface Brief: PromoterLab public atlas

## Scope and visitor mode

Public homepage; Persuade and teach.

## Audience, job, and action

Researchers and learners want to understand promoter prediction, inspect an illustrative sequence, and open the classifier workspace. The primary action is to start a prediction in the dashboard.

## Proof and constraints

Use actual sequence interaction and motif examples; avoid unverified model claims, pricing, and social proof. Dashboard is accessible without a sign-in requirement.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Riso Lab Atlas: a horizontal scientific expedition from sequence input through motif marking and promoter outcome, using paper, spot inks, and printed annotation.

## Unresolved decisions

Exact model scope and validation claims remain unconfirmed.
